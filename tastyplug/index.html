TastyPlug

A wavez.fm extension by Tastycat.

Install TastyPlug

Installing

  1. Install a userscript manager: Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey.
  2. Open the TastyPlug userscript and confirm the install.
  3. Open a wavez.fm room. The TastyPlug panel appears over the stage.

TastyPlug updates itself; there is no need to reinstall.

  • TastyPlug runs on room pages. If you move into a room from another wavez page without a page load, reload the room once.
  • You must be signed in. As a guest, chat shows Must be logged in to use tastyplug! and TastyPlug waits until you sign in.

The panel

ActionHow

Collapse / expandClick the title, or the - / + button at its right
Turn a feature on or offClick its name: blue is on, red is off
Feature settingsClick the cog on the feature's row
ResetClick Reset in the footer, then Confirm within 3 seconds

Reset puts every setting back to its default, including the panel position, and keeps your Chat Mentions word list. The panel follows the room's colour theme, and your settings are saved in your browser.

Features

Features are grouped under Auto, Alerts and Staff. Everything starts switched off.

Auto

Auto Woot - Woots every track for you, after a random delay of 1 to 10 seconds. It does not woot your own track or override a meh you cast yourself.

Auto Join - Keeps you in the DJ queue. Whenever you are not in the queue, it joins you, unless the queue is locked or full.

Alerts

Booth Alert - Warns you shortly before your turn to DJ, with a short chime:
[Booth Alert] It's almost your turn to DJ! Make sure to pick a song!

  • Spot (1-99, default 3): the queue spot at which to alert.
  • Chime (default on): play the chime with the alert.

With History Alert also on, Booth Alert checks your next song first. In the Tastycat room the check is Tastybot's OP check:
[Booth Alert] It's almost your turn to DJ! Next song: Not OP | All Time: 3 | Last Played: 5 days ago
In other rooms it is wavez's own recently played check:
[Booth Alert] It's almost your turn to DJ! Next song: Recently played | Last Played: 45 minutes ago

History Alert - Checks songs against the room's play history (the last 80 plays). With Booth Alert on, it adds your next song's history to the alert. For staff outside the Tastycat room, it also chimes when a new song starts that is in the history:
This song is on the history! (played N songs ago)

Chat Mentions - Plays the chime when someone else's message contains one of your words or phrases. Matching ignores case and punctuation. Words must be at least 3 letters.

Join Notifs. - Posts <name> joined the room when chosen kinds of user join.

  • Ranks: wavez admins and ambassadors, and the room's host, co-hosts, managers, bouncers and resident DJs.
  • Friends: people you follow.
  • Level 1s (staff only): level 1 users, shown as [Lvl 1] <name> joined the room.
Staff

Meh Tracker - Posts <name> meh'd the song! the first time each person mehs the current track.

Always on

TastyPlug chat lines - TastyPlug's alerts and results appear in the room chat as lines from TastyPlug. Only you see them.

Staff badge - In the Tastycat room, Tastycat staff get the Tastycat badge in chat, the People list and on stage. Only TastyPlug users see it.

Chat commands

Type these in the room's message box. Nothing is posted to the room. Tastycat room only.

CommandChecks
/opcheck playlistThe next song in your active playlist
/opcheck <link>A YouTube or SoundCloud link
/opcheckThe song playing now; bouncer and above

Tastybot answers privately:
[!opcheck] All Time: 3 | Month: 3 | Week: 1 | Last Played: 5 days ago | Not OP!

Rooms and ranks

  • Most features work in any wavez room.
  • Tastycat room only: the staff badge and /opcheck.
  • Staff means bouncer and above. Resident DJs and VIPs are not staff.
  • Staff only: Meh Tracker, Join Notifs' Level 1s, and History Alert's new-song alert.

Privacy

  • Settings are stored in your browser only.
  • Room data comes from wavez's official extension bridge. Votes and queue joins go through the bridge's own actions.
  • The panel, chat lines and badges exist only in your own browser.
  • Your playlists and the room's play history are read with your signed-in session, the same way the site does.
  • OP checks send Tastybot a private bot command and read its private reply.
  • In the Tastycat room, TastyPlug privately tells Tastybot its version once each time you enter the room, to confirm TastyPlug can reach it. Nothing is posted in chat.
  • None of these requests or commands are seen by the room.
