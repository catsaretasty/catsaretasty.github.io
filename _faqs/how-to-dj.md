---
title: How can I be the DJ?
---
I want to be the DJ and play a song I have selected but I don’t know how.


• To join the wait list, click the Join queue button. We can't add you to the wait list.


• When we are in Event mode, type !join in chat. Everyone who uses the command is added in order when the event ends. You only need to use it once.


• On Lottery Day, you need to win the Lottery to join the wait list. Stay active in the chat to have a chance to win.


• Just remember to be patient if you are waiting to join the waitlist! There are plenty of other users trying to do the same thing as you so it may take a while to join.
